# Tofu Pet — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## Overview

Tofu Pet ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

Tofu Pet collects only the following information **locally on your device**:

- **User-provided data**: Your name (optional), task titles, and reminder times
- **Sensor data**: Accelerometer, gyroscope readings (for gesture detection only)
- **Voice data**: Audio captured during long-press (for on-device speech recognition only)
- **App preferences**: Your settings (sensitivity sliders, pet size, snooze time, etc.)

### What We Don't Collect

Tofu Pet **does NOT**:

- Send any data to external servers
- Use cloud storage or sync services
- Track your location, contacts, or other personal data
- Use analytics or crash reporting with personal identifiers
- Require internet connection to function
- Share data with third parties
- Store data beyond your device

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device** using:

- **DataStore**: For user preferences and settings
- **Local database**: For tasks and reminders
- **Device file system**: For app configuration

You have full control over your data. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all associated data.

## Voice Recognition

- Voice commands are processed **on-device** using Android's `SpeechRecognizer`
- Audio is **not recorded, stored, or sent** anywhere
- Voice data is discarded immediately after processing
- You control when the mic activates (long-press only)

## Permissions

Tofu Pet requests the following Android permissions:

- **SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW**: To display the overlay
- **RECORD_AUDIO**: To listen for voice commands (only when you long-press)
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE**: To keep the overlay running
- **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM**: For precise task reminders
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: For foreground service notification
- **VIBRATE**: For haptic feedback

All permissions are optional and can be revoked at any time via Android Settings.

## Children

Tofu Pet is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
